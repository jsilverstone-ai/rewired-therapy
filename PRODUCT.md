# Rewired Therapy

## Audience

Adults with ADHD or executive-function strain. They can name what matters and still fail to start, sequence, or return. They have been shamed by streak apps and exhausted by dashboards. They do not need motivation. They need an appointed hour that is small enough to enter.

Related, not the same product: Rewired Ketamine is the clinic site. This app is the daily practice.

## Job of the interface

Operate. The person opens the app to keep the current form, plant a seed, or close the day.

## Platform

web first (mobile-width). Later adaptive (phone app).

## Brand commitments

Name: Rewired Therapy
Voice: officiant. Present tense. Short. No exclamation marks.
Promise: the app checks on you; you do not raise it.
