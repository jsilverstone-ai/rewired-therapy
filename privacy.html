Privacy Policy

Rewired Therapy
Last updated: September 25, 2026

Rewired Therapy is a daily executive-function app. It is not medical care, not psychotherapy, and not a substitute for a licensed clinician.

What stays on your phone

Tasks, schedules, results, and chat history are stored on the device you use (the phone or browser). We do not run a Rewired account server that keeps your to-do list. If you clear the app’s data or uninstall it, that local list is gone unless you exported it.

Cloud chat (My Agent)

If you turn the cloud model on and paste an API key, short messages from My Agent are sent to that provider so a reply can be generated:

  • Groq, if you choose Groq
  • xAI, if you choose xAI

Those providers receive the text of the exchange and the system instructions needed to answer. They do not receive your Google password. You can leave the cloud model off. Do not paste medical records into the chat.

Google Calendar and Gmail

The app does not read your mail or calendar until you tap Connect Google and approve access in Google’s own screen. If you approve, the app requests read-only Calendar and read-only Gmail so it can suggest items for your pile. You choose Keep or Ignore. You can disconnect Google in your Google Account permissions at any time.

Rewired does not get your Google password. Tokens stay on the device.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app may send reminders you set (knocks). Those reminders are scheduled on the device.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not build advertising profiles.
  • We do not use your list to train a public model.

Children

Rewired Therapy is intended for adults. It is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and post the new text at this URL.
