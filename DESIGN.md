# Rewired Therapy — visual system

Impeccable reads this file. Do not invent tokens outside it.

## Direction

A letterpress card and a temple bell, not a SaaS shell. Paper field, iron-gall ink, one oxidized-bronze accent. Hierarchy is type and space. Chrome is scarce.

## Color

Tinted neutrals only. Quiet must stay readable on paper and paper-2. Bell is for the keeper disc and the single primary verb — never as a page wash.

## Typography

Newsreader for rite names and questions. IBM Plex Sans for labels, tabs, buttons. No third family. No Inter. Do not put a tiny uppercase label as its own line directly above a heading.

## Spatial

8px base. Inset 24px on the device. Prefer a rule and stacked type over cards. Do not nest cards.

## Components

Keeper: 72px circle, ink stroke, inner disc in bell. Opacity is fidelity. No face.
Primary button: ink fill, paper text, 4px radius.
Ghost button: transparent, ink outline.
Ribbon row: time, serif name, kind in quiet 12px.

## Motion

160–220ms ease-out. No bounce.

## Copy

Officiant voice. Buttons are verbs: Keep the form / Smaller form / Park until next.
